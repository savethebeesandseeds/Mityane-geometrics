## What a later search would be

Another weight, another history length, or another pass over the closed knobs
does not reopen this search. A later version has to change what the loss asks
the encoder to keep: the window's direction, or the agreement between the
minute bars and the hour bars. That is a new declared comparison, not a
continuation of this one.
