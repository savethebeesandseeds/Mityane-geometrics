# CPB V15 objective comparison

Draft implementation for the user's approved fresh V4-design comparison. This
file records the interface; the actual declaration, source review, build
admission and CUDA engineering receipts must precede their dependent execution.
No source in this directory was built or executed by its preparation author.

The research question is whether replacing the encoder's variance-weighted
partial-NLL mean gradient with unit Huber1 improves retention at the same
native36 architecture and budget. The candidate adds unit Gaussian variance
calibration with detached mean error and a detached mean4 variance input. It
has the same parameters as fresh V4; it does not add an uncertainty embedding.
The control retains V4's original partial Gaussian-NLL gradient. Both retain
fresh weights, optimizer and TRAIN statistics, the fixed analytic prior, one
AdamW optimizer and one shared global clip1 operation.

The three paired initialization seeds are 945101, 945102 and 945103. Row, mask
and context streams are initialization seed plus1000, plus2000 and plus3000.
Each pair receives the same three streams. Each of six research trajectories
has exactly2048 successful updates, B8, at most4096 attempts, AdamW0.001 and
weight decay0.0001, query masking25%, extra context deletion15%, and no dropout.
Actual typed continuation at512 does not repeat updates. Initial0/final2048 are
both retained; no checkpoint, seed, coefficient or budget selection is provided.

Original files and checkpoints remain immutable. These controls are fresh
replication blocks for the new comparison, not attempts to recreate a saved
score. [provenance.json](provenance.json) identifies each copied frozen source;
the current files and binaries acquire their own reviewed path-bound identities.

## Populations and numerical boundaries

The original January2024 BTC/ETH/SOL CACHE88 inputs supply4096 TRAIN anchors,
112 TRAIN probes and32 previously used chronological VALIDATION probes. All
input/bank calibration uses TRAIN only. The unchanged market readouts fit
ridge1 and a joint four-output tanh16/Adam0.01/100-step head, with repetitions
2701/2802/2903 and their frozen D36 uint64 stream seeds. The three head repetitions
are not three independent encoder runs. No PCA or feature-width change is made.

TEMPO-5 retains masters941101/941102/941103,512 TRAIN and256 VALIDATION rows per
master, the same synthetic-TRAIN/market-TRAIN input-scaler policies and
intact/deleted views. Synthetic input scalers use only each cohort's TRAIN rows.
There is no synthetic encoder training. Both checkpoint stages receive fresh
unchanged heads. All earlier published protection floors remain unchanged;
the frozen machine declaration supplies their exact22 predicates and bindings.

The confirmation population is February2024 Spot1m BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT/SOLUSDT,
41760 rows per channel. Thirty-two timestamps are selected before download by
zero-based close-row index `floor((i+0.5)*41760/32)`, i=0..31. Each probe has
disjoint H256 legal support inside its calendar bin; the encoder consumes the
recent128 rows only. No February data supplies calibration, training, head
fitting, tuning or row replacement.

The original targets were generated by a legacy F32 data loader followed by F64
target arithmetic. `target_prepare_main.cpp parity` must first reproduce every
value, support flag and row/group ID in the original112/32 January target
archives exactly. The Node wrapper records the actual binary, numeric result,
log, source review, protocol review, sources, caches and original target pins.
The February importer refuses to open values without this passed receipt.
February targets retain this exact F32-to-F64 recipe; encoder input remains
genuine F64 CACHE88 data. Group IDs equal row IDs.

All six final checkpoints and their fixed TRAIN-fitted heads are sealed, with
independent numerical audits, before confirmation features, predictions or
outcomes are opened. Confirmation reuses the final market head arrays. It
constructs no optimizer or normalizer and performs no fit. Fixed-query MAE/NLL
is descriptive only: it supplies no gate or selection. Its Boolean query
geometry is copied from the known-VALIDATION32 plan; old row IDs/targets/support
are discarded, new support is recomputed from February observations, and every
row/channel must hide exactly one complete8x9 patch. Failure causes refusal,
not a new mask draw.

January remains development evidence. February is a separate population, so its
scores cannot be ranked directly against the historical32-window scores. Three
initialization pairs do not establish uncertainty over independent market
cohorts. Past-window readouts and reconstruction do not establish forecasting
or operation-policy utility. No automatic model promotion or Mityane application
selection is performed.

## Source, build and runtime interface

Use only the existing managed `mityane-geometrics` container and the root task
runner. Root `Makefile` includes [objective.mk](objective.mk); no standalone
environment or lifecycle definition is created here. Build products stay at
`/opt/mityane-geometrics/embeddings/build/cpb-v15-objective-001`. Selected local
artifacts stay under
`.temp/embeddings/runs/cpb-v15-objective-001/attempt-001/`.

`build-cpb-v15-objective-preparation` compiles only the target preparation tool.
`build-cpb-v15-experiment` compiles both train/extract arms and frozen readers.
`test-cpb-v15-objective-preparation` runs the bounded Node engineering fixtures.
Model-owned CUDA engineering and saved-checkpoint proof binaries are admitted
separately. Builds/tests do not by themselves admit scientific execution.

[prepare.mjs](prepare.mjs) takes `--inputs` with a
`cpb_v15_target_preparation_inputs_v1` card. It requires exact source/protocol
reviews and a passed build admission binding the `target_prepare` binary to the
same source generation. Its `admission` binds the same `protocol` and
`source_review`, with the exact phase in `admitted_phases`; the protocol review
lists that phase in `approved_phases`. The card
names ordered January cache pins, shared root, original target pins, binary,
and exclusive output/log/receipt leaves. Phase `target_precision_parity` produces
the prerequisite proof. Phase `confirmation_targets` additionally binds that
actual proof and the ordered February caches; its target archive stays blind.
The entire precision receipt, numeric result, binary, log, January inputs and
source/build/protocol/admission chain are rehashed before February caches are
opened by this wrapper, and rechecked before its receipt is sealed.

[run.mjs](run.mjs) takes `--inputs CARD --phase PHASE --seed N --arm ARM` for
`train`, `development` or `confirmation`, or `--inputs CARD --phase seal`.
The card kind is `cpb_v15_objective_run_inputs_v1`. It binds the actual admission,
resumption, declaration, input manifest, source/protocol reviews, build admission,
and actual CUDA engineering review. Build admission maps every binary role to
the reviewed source. Engineering review binds the same source/build generation
and actual paired initialization/objective/gradient-route checks.

The protocol review's `protocol` and `source_review` pins must match the card;
the admission binds the same declaration and admitted phases. The input manifest
kind is `cpb_v15_objective_input_manifest_v1`, whose `inputs` bind every runtime
January/shared/TEMPO/settings role. The runner enforces the fixed seed, budget,
width, coefficient, mask-geometry and confirmation boundaries before launching.
It hashes declared bytes without decoding tensors; C++ owns scientific arrays.
No Python executable or fallback exists in this protocol.

Each subrun has the fixed path
`subruns/seed-945101/control/` or `candidate/`, followed by the other two seeds.
Its leaves are `training/`, `proof-initial/`, `proof-final/`, `features/`,
`heads-initial/{market,synthetic}/` and `heads-final/{market,synthetic}/`.
Actual initial/final checkpoint proofs must pass the128cutoff/2350suffix/
30internal causal matrices and bind the checkpoint SHA, actual initialization
seed, gradient policy and update count before development extraction/readouts.
Every development phase first requires the exact passed training receipts and
outputs for all six trajectories. A passed phase receipt must bind the whole
input-card path/byte/hash tuple, phase, initialization seed and arm.

The root-owned independent arithmetic audit records each subrun as
`independent-audit.json`, kind `cpb_v15_independent_numeric_audit_v1`, passed,
with `independent_arithmetic:true`, `producer_module_forwards:0`,
`producer_arithmetic_called:false` and exact `checked_files` byte bindings.
The decoded numeric subset and metadata/hash-only checks must remain explicit;
binding a whole checkpoint does not claim a second model computation.
Only then can `seal` create the exclusive `development-seal.json`, binding all
six runs and their complete checkpoint/head output generations. Confirmation
rehashes the twelve phase receipt pins, six audit pins and all sealed outputs.
Its final checkpoint and three saved TRAIN fits must match entries in that
seal. Confirmation cannot modify or refit its inputs.

Market fits are at `heads-final/market/models/native36/rep-2701/fit.pt` (and
2802/2903). They retain original F64 feature/target normalization, ridge and
neural arrays, typed support/counts and the original signed-int64 bit pattern of
the D36 uint64 head seed. Confirmation predictions use the same keys as the
development archives: `ridge_predictions`, `neural_predictions`, `valid`,
`targets`, `target_valid`, `row_ids`, `group_ids`.

TEMPO fits are at
`heads-final/synthetic/master-941101/synthetic_train/rep-2701/fit.pt`, with all
three masters, two scaler modes and three repetitions. Saved outer/probe
normalizers, linear/tanh arrays, logits, hidden preactivations, class decisions,
validity and source IDs support independent replay and tie checks.

Every output leaf and receipt is exclusive. Failed/partial attempts, logs and
artifacts remain in place. A failure supplies no rescue seed, overwrite, cleanup
or claim of completion. New repairs require retained source/protocol identities.
Final reporting must retain every pair, earlier scientific reference, failed
condition, actual compute cost and independent audit limitation.
