## Input contract

One window is one instrument. The tensor is `[batch, 3, 128, 9]`. Channel
order is 1m, 1h, 1d. The anchor is the 1-minute channel. Coarser bars are
held as-of on that clock. Feature order is open, high, low, close, volume,
quote volume, trades, taker buy base, taker buy quote. Under the log-return
view, fields 0–3 are the log return to the previous valid close of the same
channel, and fields 4–8 are log1p. The view is applied once, in
`history_from_edge_samples(..., log_returns=true)`, against a raw cache
written with policy `none`. A second logarithm is wrong.

The population is January 2024 Binance spot monthly klines for BTCUSDT,
ETHUSDT, and SOLUSDT. TRAIN is days 1–21, close-time bounds
1704067259999 through 1705881599999, 1024 anchors, 3072 rows. Validation is
days 22–28, bounds 1705881659999 through 1706486399999, 32 anchors, 96 rows.
Row id is `instrument * 1000000000000000 + close`, instruments ordered BTC,
ETH, SOL. Days 29–31 are unused. February and March stay closed.

The window builder is
`code/data/market/experiments/timeframe_published_bars_v1/windows.cpp`.
It requests 129 minute bars, 6 hourly bars, and 3 daily bars, then left-pads
to the minute sample. A coarse channel that cannot serve the minute cutoff
is fully masked. The prepared archives are
`.temp/embeddings/runs/timeframe-published-2026-10-29/data/calibration-TRAIN.pt`
(98750992 bytes, SHA256
`000033e1ab093852af5ac7a5f6249b4f1ea4a24b155d36c6b394e175bfa36cd0`) and
`probe-VALIDATION.pt` (3090448 bytes, SHA256
`50b5f78959dfb02ad28dd15b631b38149d886f247149af39582a152228cd9d18`).
`preparation.json` records `promotes_reference: false` and
`confirmation_bound: false`.

Do not train on `timeframe_completed_bar_v1` or campaign
`timeframe-input-2026-10-29`. That path built coarser bars by aggregating
1-minute files. This run uses the publisher's own 1m, 1h, and 1d files.

Anchor coverage is 1 on all 96 rows. Width 128 mean absolute error is
0.0001719899183859668 for volatility, 0.0000520150550832486 for trend, and
0.1091288727075006 for activity. The targets are statistics of the same
128-minute window the encoder reads. Volatility is the standard deviation of
the minute log returns, trend is their mean, and activity is the mean log
volume. Activity sits at the ceiling because that volume is already in the
window. Trend is the weak readout: about a fifth of the variance of the
average minute return is linearly readable.

`cross_resolution` abstains when a coarser channel has fewer than 8 distinct
held bars. A 128-minute window holds about two hourly bars and one daily bar,
so every validation row abstained. The hour and day channels were in the
training tensor. This screen has no score for whether the encoder used them.

These four names are not inputs to `decision.mjs`. Do not place them in
`past_volatility`, `past_trend`, `past_activity`, or `past_relation`. The
2026-10-29 log-return screen `cpb-family-logreturn-v1` applied raw-price
`past_window_targets` to an already logged window. Those scores stay on that
pairing. Seed 946103 was not trained or scored on this contract, so this
table is not a ranking against it.
