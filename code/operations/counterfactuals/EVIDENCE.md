# OR-06 Requirements-and-Evidence Record

**Record:** `OR06-PCR-RER`, revision `0.1.0`, 19 October 2026.  
**Operation:** [OR06-PCR / paired-controlled-response, revision 0.1.0](CONTRACT.md).  
**Purpose:** reusable requirements and current evidence boundary. Each admitted
study supplies a versioned instance record referencing this RER and binding the
fields below. This record is not an executable study protocol.

## 1. Hypothesis and claim

The selected claim level is `controlled_model_response`. A future study tests
whether a fixed representation/response pipeline exhibits the prespecified
response to a declared alternative, with the declared support and stability.
The first [bound synthetic instance](evaluation/synthetic_response_v1/RER.md)
now supplies a frozen hypothesis, population, changes, comparisons and numerical
criteria. Other future instances remain unbound until separately admitted.
There is no hypothesis about identified real-system intervention effects in
this revision.

## 2. Data and provenance

The instance record must bind source snapshot/license/access and retention,
ObservationRefs and pair roster, unit/group identities, partitions, duplicates
and shared-source overlap, actual provider/checkpoint, SpaceRefs/geometry,
response-model and preprocessing artifacts, all fit/selection populations,
source/code/configuration identities, environment, seeds and temporal claim mode.
Bind the alternative constructor and retained baseline/alternative inputs,
responses, validity evidence and source-parent relationships. Unknown pretrained
or hosted-provider exposure is explicit and limits the claim.

**Current bindings:** the [initial synthetic protocol](evaluation/synthetic_response_v1/PROTOCOL.md)
selects a project-authored generator, four fixed feature providers, TRAIN-only
OLS readouts, declared scalar alternatives and actual retained byte identities.
It selects no inherited encoder/checkpoint or external provider. The run-bound
source capsule preserves the original prospective records independently of
later reporting edits.

## 3. Controls and baselines

Before execution, select applicable controls and justify omissions:

- A no-change pair to check the declared numerical/stochastic tolerance.
- A known changed-input control and an invalid/out-of-support alternative.
- Raw-input or simple descriptor response comparison with matched accessible
  information, alternative definitions and capacity/compute budget.
- A constant/no-response baseline; random or alternative representations and a
  conventional task baseline where informative for the actual hypothesis.

Engineering controls of contrast arithmetic, identity and refusal behavior
establish mechanics only. A model reacting to a change does not establish that
the reaction is useful, semantically valid or causally correct.

## 4. Evaluation protocol

The instance freezes development/calibration/assessment partitions, construction
and selection policy, pair eligibility, response axes/units, support tests and
thresholds, fit permissions, budgets, repeats/seeds and artifact/evidence
locations before protected assessment. Diagnostic exposure and repeated looks
are logged; revisions require fresh confirmation or an explicit exploratory
scope. Dependent windows/entities/trajectories determine the evaluation and
resampling unit. As-of eligibility follows full feature/fit dependency intervals,
not just event timestamps; future outcomes are evaluator-only.

The instance also records actual execution permissions and purpose, admission
checks and blockers, and a pre-execution attempt record. An operation-local
versioned manifest/index may satisfy discovery until a shared registry is
adopted. Preserve existing historical records by reference. Finalize every
attempt, including rejected admission, failure, interruption and abstention,
with its distinct disposition and any permitted artifact limitations.

**Current protocol:** OR06-SYNTH-RESP-001 v1.0.0 was prospectively frozen and
executed under its actual admission. Reusable documents alone continue to grant
no execution admission to another instance.

## 5. Measures of success

Contract conformance requires correct pair/axis alignment, the specified sign
and units, finite arithmetic, retained provenance, typed refusal and complete
denominator accounting. Numerical tolerances and thresholds are bound before
execution; documentation review supplies no numerical test result.

Scientific success additionally requires a predeclared primary metric and
baseline comparison/threshold for the actual hypothesis. Report effect/contrast
summaries only at the declared unit and population, with coverage and failure
rates. A stability or nonzero-response metric alone cannot certify counterfactual
validity. A valid negative or inconclusive evaluation can complete its scoped
work without supporting a positive utility claim.

## 6. Robustness and uncertainty

Predeclare sensitivity to change magnitude/direction, held-fixed constraints,
support boundaries, seeds, stochastic model calls, representation/geometry
revision, groups and population shift as applicable. Bind dependence-aware
uncertainty for any aggregate, its estimation unit and repetitions. Per-pair
deterministic arithmetic supplies no automatic confidence interval; unavailable
uncertainty and the resulting limitation must be explicit. Balance/overlap and
negative controls remain diagnostics, not causal-identification certificates.

## 7. Results and limitations

The [contract review](reviews/contract-v0.1.0.md) records the first milestone's
authoring acceptance. The [C++ reference implementation](IMPLEMENTATION.md)
now supplies an executable typed interface for the deterministic recorded-response
slice. Its [source-bound verification](reviews/reference-v0.1.0.md) records
47 passed engineering conformance cases. Those fixtures establish no scientific
utility or inherited/external provider result. Supplied
metadata and attestations are checked for internal consistency; their truth,
artifact bytes and actual upstream executions require an admitting adapter's
verification.

The [callable recorded-response tool](TOOL.md) now verifies actual referenced
file bytes and response-value bindings around that same numerical API. Its
[engineering verification](reviews/tool-v1.md) records 17 passed boundary checks,
two artificial provider identities, a generic non-pair adapter fixture and the
Windows command workflow. Other upstream semantic/model-execution attestations
remain supplied evidence; the new interface adds no scientific utility claim.

The separately admitted [synthetic results](evaluation/synthetic_response_v1/RESULTS.md)
now establish scoped end-to-end response fidelity and refusal behavior, with
zero independent audit errors. Mixed source-unit MAE was 0.000762435, tied with
the equivalent-capacity raw-polynomial control; lossy and zero baselines were
0.302927979. All supported pairs succeeded and all unsupported stress pairs
abstained. This result is limited to its finite synthetic population and fitted
models; reference fixtures alone did not establish it.

For other future instances, remaining bindings are hypothesis/population, permitted data,
provider and response artifacts, alternative validity/support criteria,
provider/loader conformance, evaluation thresholds, run protocol and permissions.
Each blocks its dependent execution until supplied; reusable
engineering work may proceed independently. Scientific compatibility cannot be
inferred from an encoder's historical scores or a shared dependency.
