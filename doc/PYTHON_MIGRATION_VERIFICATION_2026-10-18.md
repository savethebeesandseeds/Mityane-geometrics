## Limits

These are engineering and artificial scientific fixtures. They do not establish
representation/model quality or numerical equivalence on real historical saved
runs. New Node JSON/report bytes have their own encoding and source identities;
they do not replace historical receipt hashes. Retained Python arithmetic and
frozen codec pins remain authoritative for their existing scientific contracts.

The optional C++ reader/math foundations remain separate candidates; real
historical-format and numerical parity would require a separately admitted
protocol. Changed Python reader paths use new SOURCE fixture records in isolated
directories; historical failure/repair evidence and old fixture seals remain
unchanged. The frozen archive and inherited historical artifact trees were
preserved. The pinned image and its Python packages were not removed or changed.
