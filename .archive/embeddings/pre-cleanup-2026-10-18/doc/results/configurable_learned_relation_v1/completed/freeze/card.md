## Question and candidate

Can waveform reconstruction training make the learned portion of native32
expose slow cross-channel timing to the unchanged classifiers, while retaining
the useful complete embedding and reconstruction quality?

The separately tagged design is **RPB-v21**; this experiment's run group is
**RPB-v21.alt-01**. It wraps the preserved configurable RPB-v20 module. A
mask-aware bank of ordered lag comparisons feeds a bias-free, zero-initialized
learned projection into the first20 native coordinates. The final12 fixed
spectral coordinates, existing backbone and decoder architecture are retained.
The new bank uses original tick lags1/2/4/8, canonical semantic channel pairs,
all ordered feature pairs, centered odd/even normalized covariance and common
support fractions. Its precise arithmetic and layout are bound by the captured
model source and typed checkpoint architecture ID. It receives only visible
observations; timing labels and hidden target values never enter the bank.

The zero projection draws no initialization RNG. With the declared seed, every
initialized native32 value and validity flag must exactly equal the saved v20
control on all three views before readout reuse is permitted. Learning gains
are measured against this initialized control; the engineered relation bank
remains an architectural choice, not evidence of universally learned structure.
