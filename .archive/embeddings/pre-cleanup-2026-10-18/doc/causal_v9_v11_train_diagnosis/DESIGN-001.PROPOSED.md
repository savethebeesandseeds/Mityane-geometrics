# PROPOSED — saved TRAIN information diagnosis for V9 and V11

This is read-only planning. It declares no encoder, prospective machine card,
execution admission, learned state, new measurement or next mechanism. CPB-v3
remains the clean baseline and sole DEV encoder. The exact V11 2048/B8 recipe is
closed. Every source, checkpoint, result, failure and scoped record stays intact.
The parent research objective remains a better clean causal encoder protecting
all accumulated strengths. This diagnosis is one bounded step toward choosing a
later architecture, not a reduced definition of that objective.

The question is whether V11's four-mean/four-centered-variance intermediate rows
change the information usable by the existing TRAIN readouts, the contribution
of the served native blocks, and the saved reconstruction/residual routes,
relative to V9's eight-mean export. Both trained from fresh initialization under
the same V9 detached-error variance objective. Known quality motivates this
question; TRAIN algebra cannot identify a cause of validation changes.

The proposed design reuses the already independently audited V9 TRAIN output.
It computes new V11 information descriptors and saved-array contribution algebra.
It does not repeat V8/V9 diagnostic panels, V11's completed local-adjoint audit,
learning/gauge/Hadamard trust checks, or any model/head implementation.

not independent cohorts. The 32 known VALIDATION windows and all TEST/stress
populations are outside numeric scope. Synthetic TEMPO-5 files remain preserved
but are excluded from this bounded market-route diagnosis; no timing explanation
or new timing quality is implied.

V11 states and all three existing repetitions, report TRAIN R², physical MAE/RMSE
and coverage using saved ridge/neural predictions. Compare V11 results with the
cached V9 TRAIN results, not validation or old V8. Retain all repetitions and the
fixed early56/late56 descriptions. These are in-sample fits on TRAIN112 and are
not new quality or independent retraining uncertainty. No neural forward or

Cross-version differences of descriptive scalars do not align latent bases or

**Contribution of the already fitted ridge.** Explicitly declare manual saved

with cached V9 descriptors. Do not remove a block, construct an ablation predictor,

**Reconstruction and the served mean4 route.** Reuse all112 saved TRAIN fixed-query
component rows; keep MAE, reporting NLL, Huber1 and detached-error variance NLL

The saved variance and native uncertainty4 are descriptive observations. No

Prespecify descriptive associations between each fixed TRAIN row's reconstruction
components and its saved readout residuals, using all eligible rows and all four

turn these associations into causal validation claims, or use them as a new head
selection/tuning criterion. Cached V9 reconstruction/residual descriptors form
reference context; cross-model gradient cosines are excluded.

**What the V11 intermediate moment routes actually carried.** Use all eight
subgroups and four fixed 512-update blocks to describe their recorded clipped
gradient and movement magnitudes. This is new descriptive allocation across the
mean4/variance4 partition; the completed event/partition/learning trust checks
are cited, not rerun. Movement includes AdamW decay. Magnitudes depend on the
coordinate units and parameterization, so they cannot rank gradient dominance,
information importance or a hypothetical alternate clipping policy.

At both actual updates, use both ordinary/contextual saved routes without
selecting the active one. The proposed fields are packed positions/valid/rows;
bin-valid, bin-mean, bin-variance and mixture weights; retained PRECLIP export
upstream and its explicit defined flag; saved before-step export weight/bias;
and update/attempt/clip factor. Original hidden-state arrays are not numerically
needed. Undefined upstream with its declared zero fallback is not a dead branch.

New scalar summaries may describe support, moment distributions, mixture
concentration and same-forward export contributions for the existing four mean
rows versus four variance rows in every bin. Any export projection arithmetic
must be labelled manual saved affine contractions with their fixed row slices
and before-step weights. No alternate forward, branch deletion, reweighting,
counterfactual loss, hidden/backbone Jacobian, adjoint parity recheck or optimizer
step is permitted. This separates use of a captured moment from the unsupported
claim that it caused the activity/relation/timing gains. V9 lacks matching moment
traces; no paired V9/V11 intermediate-moment effect estimate is available.

## What is missing and will stay missing

- V9 did not capture V11's moment/attention/upstream trace fields. They cannot be
  recreated by running V9 or synthesized from cached native40 summaries.
- V11's fixed-query archives do not capture intermediate moments/activations
  for all112 TRAIN readout rows. The two actual minibatches do not identify a
  native moment-to-readout route for those112 rows or validate a per-task cause.
- The matching state2047 decoder is not saved for actual update2048. Final2048
  decoder coefficients must not be paired with that pre-step actual witness.
- Raw per-update full gradients, branch split norms and optimizer counterfactuals
  are unavailable for the other2046 steps. CSV observations are not reconstructed
  missing tensors. Existing local-adjoint proofs remain their original scope.
- Saved intermediate checkpoints are preserved, but missing feature/head/query
  captures at those states will not be created through old inference or refitting.
- One encoder trajectory per version does not provide replication uncertainty.
  No new numeric gate, candidate, coefficient, seed, learning rate, budget,
  checkpoint selection or promised next architecture follows from this plan.
