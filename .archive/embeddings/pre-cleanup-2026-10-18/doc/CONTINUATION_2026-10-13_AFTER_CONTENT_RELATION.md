## Conditional ideas kept in reserve

Masked-view consistency is not needed to repair this passed recipe. Historical
v9 full-native agreement remains stopped. If a future challenge exposes a new
robustness failure while learned timing is useful, design a separate learned-only
denoising experiment against a frozen informative teacher. Use two masks of the
same exact row/variant, never force opposite-timing source partners to agree,
and never expose hidden reconstruction targets to the teacher.

Avoid calibrating an auxiliary loss from tiny random initialization variances.
Any loss scale, coefficient and stability guard needs a new TRAIN/artificial-only
design, bounded component gradients, representation-spread checks and a frozen
prospective card. Reconstruction alone does not prove noncollapse. Do not reopen
v9 or v21 through coefficient, rate, head, seed or budget tuning.
