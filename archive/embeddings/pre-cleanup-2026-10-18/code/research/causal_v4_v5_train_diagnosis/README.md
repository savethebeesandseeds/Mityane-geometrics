diagnose.py writes an exclusive diagnosis.json or preserved failure.json. The
card must bind root's final V5 broad preservation receipt before either call.

separate uncertainty4 basis. All 112 TRAIN feature/prediction rows, existing

arithmetic. All four tasks and three saved neural repetitions are retained;
no neural weights or forward, new fit or block-ablation predictor is used.

variance-to-mean4 is detached, so its non-applied sensitivity is hypothetical.
